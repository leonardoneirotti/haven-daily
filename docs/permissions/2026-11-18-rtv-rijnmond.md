## His reply (original, Dutch)

> Dank voor je mail en leuk om te lezen waar je mee bezig bent! Mooi initiatief. Wat mij betreft staan we hier positief tegenover. We vinden het belangrijk dat onze journalistiek zoveel mogelijk mensen bereikt en dat er vanuit jouw platform wordt verwezen naar Rijnmond. **In English** (Claude's translation, for readers of this repo; the Dutch text above is what counts):

> Thanks for your email, and nice to read what you're working on! A good initiative. As far as I'm concerned, we are positive about this. We think it's important that our journalism reaches as many people as possible and that your platform refers to Rijnmond. A short summary in your own words, with a clear source credit and a link to the full article, sounds like a fine starting point in principle. I am still curious how you select the articles and make the summaries. Is that done manually or automatically, for example with the help of AI? We would also like to agree that the use stays non-commercial and that any expansion is discussed with us first.

- **Agreed to both conditions:** the use stays non-commercial, and any expansion is checked with RTV Rijnmond first.
