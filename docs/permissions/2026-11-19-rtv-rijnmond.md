## His reply

> We're happy to support your initiative and are fine with AI-generated summaries, provided that:
>
> - Summaries are limited to one or two sentences, written in your own words.
> - Rijnmond is always clearly credited, with a direct link to the original article.
> - The summaries accurately reflect our reporting, without changing the context or meaning.
> - The project remains strictly non-commercial.
> - Any expansion or changes to the use of our content are discussed with us in advance.
> - Rijnmond reserves the right to withdraw its permission at any time.
>
> Please also make sure it's clear that Haven Daily is an independent project and that Rijnmond is not responsible for the AI-generated summaries.

## The owner's answer (summary, 19 Nov 2026)

- **Agreed to all conditions.**
- Asked whether the **impact line for planners** is also okay for Rijnmond items. **Answered 23 Nov 2026: yes, on conditions** (`2026-11-23-rtv-rijnmond.md`).
